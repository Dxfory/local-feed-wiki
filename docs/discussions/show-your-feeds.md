# Show your feeds

Share a list of public feeds.

Use `https://` URLs only. Do not include tokens. Do not include private feeds.

Copy the template, replace each `https://example.com/...` address with a public feed, and post the filled block.

```text
# Public feeds

- title: Example feed
  url: https://example.com/feed.xml

- title: Example notes
  url: https://example.com/notes.xml
```
